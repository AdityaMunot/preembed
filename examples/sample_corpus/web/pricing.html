Pricing

The Team plan costs 12 dollars per user per month and includes unlimited projects, shared dashboards, and email support with a one business day response target.

The Enterprise plan adds single sign-on, audit log export, a dedicated account manager, and a contractual uptime commitment of 99.9 percent measured monthly.

Billing

  • Invoices are issued on the first day of each month.
  • Annual billing receives a 15 percent discount.
  • Seats can be added at any time and are prorated to the end of the period.
